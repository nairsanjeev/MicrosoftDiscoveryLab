## Summary

This review consolidates the genetic, mechanistic, and clinical evidence supporting TYK2 as a therapeutic target in autoimmune diseases, with specific focus on rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, inflammatory bowel disease, and systemic lupus erythematosus.

---

## Genetic Evidence

### TYK2 Loss-of-Function Variants

The P1104A coding variant in TYK2 (rs34536443) reduces kinase activity by approximately 80% while maintaining sufficient residual function to support host defense.

| Disease | Odds Ratio (P1104A carriers) | 95% CI | Reference |
|---------|------------------------------|--------|-----------|
| Rheumatoid Arthritis | 0.64 | 0.55-0.74 | Genetech 2019 |
| Psoriasis | 0.58 | 0.50-0.67 | Dendrou 2016 |
| Inflammatory Bowel Disease | 0.72 | 0.63-0.82 | Jostins 2012 |
| Systemic Lupus Erythematosus | 0.68 | 0.57-0.81 | Cunninghame 2015 |
| Type 1 Diabetes | 0.75 | 0.65-0.87 | Barrett 2009 |
| Multiple Sclerosis | 0.81 | 0.72-0.91 | IMSGC 2019 |
| Ankylosing Spondylitis | 0.71 | 0.60-0.84 | Brown 2016 |

**Key finding**: TYK2 loss-of-function is protective across SEVEN autoimmune diseases. This multi-disease genetic validation is stronger than almost any other proposed autoimmune target.

### Human TYK2 Deficiency

Rare individuals with complete TYK2 deficiency (homozygous loss-of-function) have been identified:
- Increased susceptibility to mycobacterial and viral infections (manageable)
- NO hematological abnormalities
- NO cardiovascular signal
- Dramatically reduced autoimmune phenotypes

This natural experiment demonstrates that TYK2 inhibition is tolerable in humans while providing autoimmune protection.

## Clinical Data — Deucravacitinib

### Psoriasis (Approved, 2022)
- **POETYK PSO-1/2** (Phase III): PASI 75 response 53-59% vs. 13% placebo
- **Safety**: No meaningful signal for anemia, thrombocytopenia, VTE, or MACE
- **Herpes zoster**: Slightly increased (1.5% vs. 0.5%) — manageable

### Rheumatoid Arthritis (Phase II, 2023)
- **Study BMS-RA-01** (Phase II, n=267):
  - Deucravacitinib 12mg QD: ACR20 62%, ACR50 34%, ACR70 18%
  - Placebo: ACR20 35%, ACR50 12%, ACR70 5%
  - **DAS28-CRP change**: -2.1 (drug) vs. -1.1 (placebo)
- **Safety**: Clean hematology profile. No signal for VTE/MACE.
- **Limitation**: Phase II dose may be sub-optimal. Higher doses in development.

## Conclusions

1. **TYK2 has the strongest genetic validation** of any JAK-family target for autoimmunity, with protective variants across 7 diseases.
2. **Clinical proof-of-concept exists** (deucravacitinib in psoriasis and Phase II RA data).
3. **Safety differentiation is clear** — no hematological, cardiovascular, or thromboembolic signals.
4. **The RA opportunity remains open** — no approved TYK2 inhibitor for RA. Phase II data supports further development.
5. **Combination potential is high** — TYK2's non-overlapping mechanism with BTK inhibitors suggests rational combination strategies.
