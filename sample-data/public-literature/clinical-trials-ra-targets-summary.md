## TYK2 Inhibitor Trials in Rheumatoid Arthritis

### NCT04999839 (Simulated) — Deucravacitinib Phase II in RA
- **Sponsor**: Bristol-Myers Squibb
- **Status**: Completed (October 2023)
- **Phase**: Phase II
- **Design**: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled
- **Population**: Moderate-to-severe RA, inadequate response to methotrexate (MTX-IR)
- **Arms**: Deucravacitinib 6mg QD, 12mg QD, 12mg BID vs. placebo (all on background MTX)
- **Primary endpoint**: ACR20 at Week 12
- **Results**:
  - 6mg QD: ACR20 48% (p=0.04 vs placebo)
  - 12mg QD: ACR20 62% (p<0.001 vs placebo)
  - 12mg BID: ACR20 67% (p<0.001 vs placebo)
  - Placebo: ACR20 35%
- **Key secondary**: DAS28-CRP remission at Week 24: 18% (12mg QD) vs. 5% (placebo)
- **Safety**: No meaningful hematological AEs. Herpes zoster: 2 cases in drug arms vs. 0 placebo.

### NCT05234567 (Simulated) — Deucravacitinib Phase III in RA (POETYK RA-1)
- **Sponsor**: Bristol-Myers Squibb
- **Status**: Recruiting (started March 2025)
- **Phase**: Phase III
- **Design**: Randomized, double-blind, active-controlled (adalimumab comparator)
- **Population**: Moderate-to-severe RA, MTX-IR
- **Arms**: Deucravacitinib 12mg QD vs. Adalimumab 40mg Q2W vs. Placebo
- **Primary endpoint**: ACR50 at Week 24
- **Estimated completion**: Q4 2027

### NCT05678901 (Simulated) — NDI-034858 (Nimbus/Takeda) Phase II in RA
- **Sponsor**: Takeda
- **Status**: Active, not yet recruiting (planned Q3 2025)
- **Phase**: Phase II
- **Mechanism**: TYK2 catalytic site (JH1) inhibitor — different mechanism from deucravacitinib
- **Population**: Moderate-to-severe RA, bDMARD-IR
- **Primary endpoint**: ACR20 at Week 12

## BTK Inhibitor Trials in Rheumatoid Arthritis

### NCT03865927 (Simulated) — Fenebrutinib Phase II in RA
- **Sponsor**: Roche/Genentech
- **Status**: Completed (2020)
- **Phase**: Phase II
- **Results**:
  - 200mg BID: ACR50 35% vs. 15% placebo (p<0.001)
  - ACR70: 18% vs. 6% placebo
- **Key finding**: Response correlated with seropositivity (RF+/CCP+). Seropositive patients: ACR50 42% vs. 12% placebo.
- **Safety**: Well-tolerated. No A-fib or bleeding events. Mild transaminase elevations in 4%.

### NCT04305444 (Simulated) — Fenebrutinib Phase III in RA (FENtastic-1)
- **Sponsor**: Roche/Genentech
- **Status**: Completed (March 2024)
- **Phase**: Phase III
- **Results**: MISSED primary endpoint (ACR50 at Week 24 vs. placebo in all-comers)
  - All-comers: ACR50 32% vs. 24% placebo (p=0.06 — NOT significant)
  - Pre-specified subgroup (seropositive): ACR50 39% vs. 20% (p=0.002 — significant)
- **Interpretation**: BTK inhibition works in seropositive RA but not in unselected populations.

### NCT04674566 (Simulated) — Evobrutinib Phase II in RA
- **Sponsor**: Merck KGaA
- **Status**: Completed (2021)
- **Results**: Did not meet primary endpoint (ACR20 at Week 12). Nominal significance at Week 24.
- **Post-hoc**: Seropositive patients showed signal (ACR20 55% vs. 38%)

### NCT05912345 (Simulated) — BTK Inhibitor + TYK2 Inhibitor Combination (Phase I)
- **Sponsor**: Undisclosed pharma
- **Status**: Planned (not yet recruiting)
- **Phase**: Phase I (PK/PD/safety)
- **Rationale**: Address both B-cell and IFN/Th17 axes simultaneously
- **Population**: Healthy volunteers → RA patients in expansion
- **Note**: First trial to explore this specific combination

## JAK2-Selective Inhibitor Trials (RA-Relevant)

### No JAK2-selective inhibitors currently in clinical development for RA

**Reason**: Known hematological toxicity (anemia, thrombocytopenia) at efficacious doses makes JAK2-selective inhibition unviable as RA monotherapy.

**Related trial**:
- Fedratinib (JAK2-selective, approved for myelofibrosis): Hematological AEs in >60% of patients at therapeutic doses. Not pursued for autoimmune indications.

## Implications for Our Target Assessment

1. **TYK2 is clinically validated** with Phase II efficacy and clean safety. Phase III ongoing — competitive window still open if we can differentiate.
2. **BTK works in seropositive patients** — our internal data (BTK elevated in disease) is confirmed, but patient selection is critical.
3. **JAK2 is not viable as monotherapy** — consistent with our internal safety data (INT-TOX-2022-008).
4. **The TYK2 + BTK combination is being explored by competitors** — validates our combination hypothesis from the immunology analysis.
5. **First-mover advantage**: No approved TYK2 inhibitor for RA yet. A differentiated compound with better RA efficacy (higher dose or better tissue penetration) could succeed.
